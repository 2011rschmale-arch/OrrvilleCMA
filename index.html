Who We Are

A Church Family
on Mission Together

Orrville C&MA Church is a community of Christ-followers who love and trust Jesus, desiring to serve Him fully. We find answers to the struggles of this life and the way to eternal life in the Word of God.

Our roots in the Christian & Missionary Alliance bear the fruit of taking our message to a world that desperately needs to know Jesus personally. Whatever your age or stage, there is a place for you here.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

“I am the vine; you are the branches. If a man remains in me and I in him, he will bear much fruit; apart from me you can do nothing.” (John 15:5)
“Now this is eternal life: that they may know you, the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom you have sent.” (John 17:3)
“Be devoted to one another in brotherly love. Honor one another above yourselves.” (Romans 12:10)
“Therefore, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all people, especially to those who belong to the family of believers.” (Galatians 6:10)
“…to prepare God's people for works of service, so that the body of Christ may be built up.” (Ephesians 4:12)
“So it is with you. Since you are eager to have spiritual gifts, try to excel in gifts that build up the church.” (1 Corinthians 14:12)
“Therefore go and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit, and teaching them to obey everything I have commanded you. And surely I am with you always, to the very end of the age.” (Matthew 28:19–20)
