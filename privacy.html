Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026. This page explains what information this website collects and how it's used.

What we collect

If you use the contact form on our Visit page, we collect the name, email address, phone number (optional), and message you provide. That information is sent directly to the church office by email so we can respond to you — it isn't stored in a marketing database or shared with outside organizations.

Embedded content from other sites

Some pages embed content hosted elsewhere:

  • YouTube — our sermon archive and live stream are embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which limits tracking unless you press play.
  • Google Docs — our weekly bulletin is embedded directly from Google Docs.
  • Google Maps — our Visit page embeds a map to help you find us.

Each of these is operated by Google/YouTube, not by us, and is governed by their own privacy policies. Loading these pages may set cookies from those services.

Giving online

Our "Give" button takes you to EasyTithe/Amplify, a third-party giving platform, to process your donation securely. We do not receive or store your card or bank account information — that's handled entirely by their platform under their own privacy and security practices.

Cookies

This site uses one small, technical cookie of its own: a session cookie tied to the contact form, used only to limit automated spam submissions. It doesn't track you across other websites and isn't used for advertising.

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. Our downloadable kids' resources (like sermon note sheets) are provided for parents to print at home and involve no data collection at all.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, contact us at orrcma@zoominternet.net or (330) 682-7666.
