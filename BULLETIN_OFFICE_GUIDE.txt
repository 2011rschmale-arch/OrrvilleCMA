WEEKLY BULLETIN — OFFICE GUIDE

Each week, update the bookmarked Bulletin Google Doc exactly as you would any
other Word-style document. Google saves automatically. The church website will
show the updated document without any website login or upload.

Simple weekly routine:
1. Open the bookmarked Bulletin Google Doc.
2. Replace or remove last week's information.
3. Add this week's announcements, schedule, family news, and future dates.
4. Check the date and read through the document once.
5. Close the document. You are done.

Helpful reminders:
- Use regular headings, bold text, bullet lists, and links whenever useful.
- You do NOT need special all-caps headings, symbols, pipes, or website code.
- Do NOT change the document's sharing or "Publish to web" settings.
- Changes can take about 5 to 10 minutes to appear on the public website.
- Images and tables work too, but plain text, bold, headings, bullet lists and
  links display best (especially on phones).
- If the bulletin does not appear after 10 minutes, contact the web person.
