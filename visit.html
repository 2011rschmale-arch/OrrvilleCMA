First Visit FAQ

Come as you are! Our congregation dresses everything from casual to business casual. There's no dress code — what matters is that you're here.

Yes! We have nursery care for infants and toddlers, and Children's Church for kids K–2nd grade during the 10:30 AM worship service. Sunday School for all ages begins at 9:15 AM.

Our Sunday morning worship service is typically 60–70 minutes, beginning at 10:30 AM. We include worship music, Scripture reading, prayer, and a sermon.

Parking is available in our church lot off S. Walnut Street, as well as street parking nearby. Accessible parking spaces are available close to the entrance.

Absolutely. We live-stream every Sunday service at 10:30 AM on our YouTube channel (@orrvillecmachurch8192). Archived messages are also available anytime on the same channel.

The best way to get connected is to join us on Sunday morning and introduce yourself. You can also fill out our contact form above, call the church office at (330) 682-7666, or email us at orrcma@zoominternet.net. We'd love to help you find your place here.
