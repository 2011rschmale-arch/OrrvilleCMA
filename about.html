Who We Are

About Our Church

A community of Christ-followers in Orrville, Ohio — rooted in Scripture, united in worship, and sent on mission.

Our Foundation

Built on the Word of God

The purpose of this fellowship of believers is to exalt Jesus Christ through trust in the dynamic power of the indwelling Christ and through obedience to the infallible Word of God. Our mission is to make disciples.

Full families, young married couples, singles, and everyone in between will find ministry opportunities that help them enjoy a deeper and more fulfilling relationship with the living God.

We are part of the Christian & Missionary Alliance — a denomination born out of a deep passion for the fullness of Christ and the unreached peoples of the world.

Doctrine & Faith

What We Believe

Our beliefs are grounded in orthodox Christianity as expressed in the historic creeds and the C&MA Statement of Faith. We hold the Bible to be the inspired, infallible Word of God.

The Old and New Testaments, inerrant as originally given, were verbally inspired by God and are a complete and sufficient revelation of His will for the salvation of men. They constitute the only infallible rule of faith and practice.

There is one God, who is infinitely perfect, existing eternally in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. God is all-powerful, all-knowing, and present everywhere, sustaining all of creation by His sovereign will.

Jesus Christ is true God and true man, having been conceived of the Holy Spirit and born of the Virgin Mary. He died on the cross as a substitutionary sacrifice for our sins and rose bodily from the dead. He ascended to the right hand of the Father and will return personally and visibly.

The Holy Spirit is a divine person who convicts the world of sin, righteousness, and judgment. He regenerates, sanctifies, and empowers believers. The C&MA holds a distinctive conviction about the deeper life — sanctification and the fullness of the Spirit available to every believer.

Salvation has been provided through Jesus Christ for all people. Those who repent and believe in Christ are born again of the Holy Spirit, receive the gift of eternal life, and become children of God. This salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.

The Church consists of all who have been born again. Its mission is to make disciples of all nations through evangelism, worship, fellowship, and service. We observe the ordinances of Believer's Baptism and the Lord's Supper.

Our Heritage

A History of Faithfulness

1887

C&MA Founded

The Christian & Missionary Alliance was founded by A.B. Simpson with a vision for the deeper life and world missions.

1928

Orrville Church Established

The Orrville congregation was planted, bringing the C&MA movement to Wayne County, Ohio.

Mid Century

Community Growth

The church grew to serve multiple generations of Orrville families, establishing Sunday School and youth programs.

Present Day

Missions Partnership

We endeavor to bring all people into unity with Jesus Christ through meaningful worship, instruction, fellowship, and outreach.

Our Denomination

The Christian & Missionary Alliance

Founded in 1887 by A.B. Simpson, the C&MA is a denomination born out of a deep passion for the fullness of Christ and the evangelization of the world's unreached peoples.

With over 2,000 churches in the United States and 700+ International Workers in 70+ countries, the C&MA continues its mission of bringing all peoples into unity with Jesus Christ.
